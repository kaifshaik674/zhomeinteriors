OUR SERVICES & EXECUTIONS

Tailored interior solutions built with high-grade engineering materials and expert craftsmanship.

Materials: BWP Marine Plywood, Anti-skid Vitrified Tiles, Kohler/Jaquar Fittings.
Kickstart: Site Measurement ➔ 3D Layout ➔ Waterproofing ➔ Fitting.

Compact guest bathrooms designed with accent wall cladding, vanity units, and floating mirrors.

Materials: Italian Marble Counters, Quartz Vanities, Acrylic Panels.
Kickstart: Vanity Design Approval ➔ Wiring ➔ Tile & Fixture Fitting.

Formal Dining Room

Custom crockery units, pendant chandelier lighting, and paneled feature walls for elevated dining.
